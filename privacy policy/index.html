Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

UN-MARKD respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

Information we collect

When you contact UN-MARKD, request a quote or submit an enquiry, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number and details about your project.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • respond to your enquiries and quote requests;
  • contact you about UN-MARKD services;
  • provide and arrange our services;
  • communicate with you about your project.

We will only use your personal information for purposes reasonably connected with our business and your enquiry.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide our services.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Access and correction

You may contact us if you would like to access or correct personal information we hold about you.
