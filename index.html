Common Questions

Everything you
might be wondering.

What is ceramic surface protection?

Ceramic surface protection is a professionally applied invisible coating that helps protect your surfaces from everyday stains, water marks, oils and grime. It also makes regular cleaning faster and easier while helping maintain the original appearance of your surfaces.

Why should I protect my new benchtops?

Most new stone benchtops are installed without any protective coating. Applying ceramic protection early helps reduce staining, water marks and everyday wear before damage occurs.

What surfaces can be coated?

We can protect many surfaces throughout your home, including natural stone, engineered stone, porcelain, bathroom vanities, splashbacks, glass shower screens, stovetops & cooktops, stainless steel, timber and outdoor kitchens.

Does the coating change the appearance of my stone?

No. Our ceramic coating is completely invisible once cured. It won't change the colour, finish or natural beauty of your surfaces.

What does it protect against?

Our coating helps resist coffee, red wine, cooking oils, tomato sauce, beetroot, berries, turmeric, water marks and everyday grime. No coating is completely stain-proof, but it gives you more time to wipe up spills before they can penetrate the surface.

Is it food safe?

Yes. Our ceramic coating is FDA verified food safe once fully cured, making it suitable for kitchen benchtops and food preparation areas.

Is it safe for children and pets?

Yes. Once cured, the coating is safe for family homes, including children and pets.

How long does the protection last?

With proper care, professionally applied ceramic protection can last 5+ years, depending on the surface, usage and maintenance.

Does it make cleaning easier?

Absolutely. The coating creates a water- and oil-repellent surface that helps reduce the build-up of dirt, making everyday cleaning quicker and easier.
